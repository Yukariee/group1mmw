06 Classifying the Data

How each collected variable is classified for this MMW activity, based on how it was actually gathered.

Nominal

  • Main digital device used (Q3)
  • Main classroom activity on the device (Q6)

Ordinal

  • Year level (Q2)
  • Device effectiveness (Q7)
  • Distraction frequency (Q8)
  • Likert responses (Q9–Q13)

Likert responses are treated as ordinal, not interval: the categories have a clear order, but the distance between “Agree” and “Strongly Agree” is not established to equal the distance between other adjacent categories.

Ratio

  • School-related device hours per day (Q4)
  • Non-school device hours per day (Q5)

Age (Q1)

The questionnaire offered age brackets, but every respondent in this sample answered with an exact age in years (18 or 19). As collected, age is therefore treated here as ratio-level numeric data rather than forced into the original ordinal brackets.
